# *MyRasa SmartMart: Measuring Smart Retail Success*

**[▶ Open the live dashboard](https://shab-analytics.github.io/myrasa-kpi-dashboard/)**

*How will management know whether a smart retail strategy is actually working? This case file builds a KPI framework for a Malaysian grocer's smart-store pilot and shows, through an interactive dashboard, how the scale-up decision would be made.*

## *The business question*

*MyRasa SmartMart, a neighbourhood grocer with 21 outlets, has piloted smart retailing in five stores: self-checkout kiosks, a shopping app, click-and-collect, trained SmartStore Hosts and opt-in personalisation. Adoption is uneven, customers worry about privacy, and the investment has to pay. At the month-9 review, management must decide whether to scale, keep testing or pause, and needs evidence rather than impressions to do it.*

## *My approach*

- *Turned each pilot problem in the case into one measurable objective.*
- *Built a framework of six KPIs (four leading, two lagging) and four guardrails, mapped across the customer journey using the TOE framework.*
- *Used difference-in-differences against matched comparison outlets, so promotions and festive seasons are not credited to the strategy.*
- *Set green / amber / red decision rules in advance, with owners and a daily, weekly and monthly review cycle.*

## *What the work shows*

- *Success is not adoption or sales. It is customers shopping more easily, coming back, and the stores earning more after the extra costs.*
- *Trust is an operating rule, not a slogan: a breached guardrail (privacy, price accuracy, system availability or access to a person) blocks expansion, however good sales look.*
- *In the demo scenario the month-9 gate is **Green**: 5 of 6 KPIs meet target and contribution is +RM 1.9k per outlet per month above comparison stores. Customer effort misses its target, showing that the rules allow one miss but still flag what to fix.*
- *Early losses are expected: contribution dips in months 1–2 before overtaking comparison stores, which is why the decision waits until month 9.*
